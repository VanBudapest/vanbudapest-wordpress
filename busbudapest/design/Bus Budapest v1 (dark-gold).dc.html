Good to know

Frequently asked

How does airport pickup work?

We track your flight and adjust to delays. Your driver meets you in the arrivals hall with a name sign, helps with luggage and takes you straight to the vehicle. Up to 60 minutes of waiting is included after landing.

Is the price really fixed?

Yes. The price we confirm in writing is all-in — tolls, taxes and included waiting. It doesn't change with traffic or minor delays.

How do I pay?

No payment is taken on this site. We agree the method when we confirm your booking — convenient options for both travellers and companies, with an invoice available on request.

Can you handle large groups and events?

Yes — from a single minibus to multiple coaches with staggered pickups and on-site coordination. Tell us the headcount, venues and timings and we'll build the plan.

Do you drive outside Hungary?

We regularly drive cross-border across Central Europe — Vienna, Bratislava, Prague and beyond — as point-to-point transfers or multi-day itineraries.

What if my plans change?

We share clear cancellation terms before you confirm. Reach us any time by phone, WhatsApp or email and we'll do our best to adjust.
