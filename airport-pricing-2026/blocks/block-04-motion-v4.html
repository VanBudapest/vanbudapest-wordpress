Transparent Pricing

Your confirmed quote already includes highway tolls, VAT, and a personalized Meet & Greet service. Budapest Airport parking is shown as a separate, itemised line: €12 on arrival, €3 on departure. Even if your flight is delayed or arrives late at night, your transfer cost remains the same.

From 2025, Budapest Airport increased its parking and waiting fees for commercial vehicles. To maintain full transparency, we show these airport costs as a separate, itemised line instead of hiding them in the fare. The €12 arrival fee covers airport parking and up to 60 minutes of waiting time from landing, including your Meet & Greet service inside the terminal. If luggage collection or customs takes longer, we recommend contacting your chauffeur via the provided phone number so they can adjust their arrival at the meeting point accordingly. Should the waiting time exceed 60 minutes, Budapest Airport’s current parking tariff applies and can be settled directly with the driver.

Personalized Meet & Greet Service

Upon landing at Budapest Liszt Ferenc International Airport (BUD), your chauffeur will monitor your flight and enter the terminal approximately 15 minutes after landing. You’ll find your driver waiting at the Meet & Greet point inside the arrivals hall, holding a sign with your name. This service ensures a smooth connection between your flight and your private vehicle.

Our chauffeurs assist with luggage and guide you directly to your car, parked at the designated airport transfer zone. Every service includes flight monitoring, professional handling, and personal attention to ensure a stress-free arrival. For departures, our drivers drop passengers off at the short-term departure zone, where a 5-minute drop-off window applies (€3, itemised on your confirmation). This ensures convenient unloading without entering higher-cost parking zones.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find my driver at the airport?

Your chauffeur will meet you inside the arrivals hall at the designated Meet & Greet point, holding a sign with your name. They will also assist you with luggage and escort you to your vehicle.

What if my flight is delayed?

No problem – our system monitors flights in real time. Your driver adjusts pickup time automatically, and you won’t be charged extra for delays within the included waiting time.

Can I pay online or on arrival?

Yes. You can pay securely online during booking or directly to the driver in cash or card upon arrival.
