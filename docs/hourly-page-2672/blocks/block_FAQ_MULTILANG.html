Hourly rates usually apply within Budapest city limits. For destinations outside the city, a custom quote or intercity pricing applies. VanBudapest can tailor your hourly or long-distance journey as needed.

How are the total hours and cost calculated for a booking?

To account for dispatch and return travel, one extra hour is automatically added to every booking. For example, a six-hour service is billed as seven hours in total.

Hourly service includes a private, chauffeured vehicle with Meet & Greet at pickup. Chauffeurs are English-speaking, well-trained, and discreet. Vehicles are clean, air-conditioned, and equipped for premium comfort — ideal for VIPs and business travelers alike.

A private chauffeur service ensures exclusivity and pre-scheduled comfort. Unlike a taxi, there are no shared rides or unexpected stops. Rates are fixed by the hour, and your journey is planned in advance — perfect for professional, event, or sightseeing use.
