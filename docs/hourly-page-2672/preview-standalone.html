What your hourly rate covers

Price Information

Price Includes: Meet & Greet services, toll fees, all taxes, and VAT.

Parking

Please note that parking fees are not included in our prices for hourly-based services, as these costs cannot be precisely calculated in advance. Parking fees may vary depending on the zone, duration of stay, event conditions, or potential delays. Our chauffeurs always do their utmost to find a suitable location where parking is free of charge; however, this is not always possible. In such cases, parking fees must be settled on-site directly by the passenger, as they are considered variable, location-dependent costs.

Waiting time & city limits

Your driver will be waiting at the designated parking area throughout the entire program, offering you full flexibility to continue to additional destinations or return to your accommodation — or any preferred location — at your convenience. Hourly rates apply within Budapest city limits. Additional charges may apply for extended routes or out-of-town services. Please contact us for a personalized quotation if your itinerary goes beyond city limits.

The +1 garage hour

For hourly chauffeur services, please note that an additional one-hour charge is automatically applied to each booking. This extra hour covers the vehicle’s departure from and return to the central garage, which is part of the operational time. For example, if you request 6 hours of service, the total billing time will be 7 hours — 6 hours of vehicle availability as requested, plus 1 additional hour covering the departure from and return to the central garage.

How Hourly Hire Works

Clear rules, published upfront — the same for every vehicle class.

Your booking, hour by hour

  1. Minimum 3 hours with youBook any length from 3 to 12 hours. Your chauffeur stays with you the whole time and waits between stops.
  2. +1 garage hourOne hour is added to every booking for the vehicle's trip from our depot to your pickup and back. 6 hours with you = 7 hours billed.
  3. From 7 hours: lower hourly rateLonger days are priced at the lower band automatically — no code, no request needed.
  4. Extend on the dayNeed another hour? Subject to availability, we extend at the same hourly rate for your vehicle class.
