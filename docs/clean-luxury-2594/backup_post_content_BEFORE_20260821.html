Daily Vehicle Cleaning & Disinfection

• Pre and Post Ride Sanitization: Every vehicle undergoes cleaning and sanitization before and after each trip. High touch surfaces — door handles, seat belts, arm rests, steering wheel and controls — are disinfected using EPA registered, quaternary ammonium based. Visible dirt is removed with detergent and water before disinfectant is.

• Hand Sanitizer & Wipes: Hand sanitizer and disinfectant wipes are provided to passengers in every vehicle.

• Ventilation: When weather permits, chauffeurs open windows or adjust ventilation to increase fresh air. HVAC systems are regularly serviced and filters are replaced more frequently than manufacturer recommendations.

• Removal of Shared Items: Printed magazines, snacks and shared amenities have been removed from vehicles to minimize cross contact. Sealed bottled water is offered on request, and refreshments are individually.

Advanced Disinfection Technologies

• Stabilized Aqueous Ozone (SAO): Our fleet is periodically treated with stabilized aqueous ozone — a powerful chemical free solution that eliminates germs, odors, mold and mildew. SAO is more effective than bleach and kills the majority of viruses.

• Ozone Generator Treatments: Vehicles are regularly treated with an ozone generator to deodorize and remove airborne particles. Ozone (O₃) is a reactive form of oxygen that alters the chemical composition of contaminants; however, it can cause respiratory irritation in occupied spaces. We run ozone treatments only when vehicles are unoccupied and follow industry recommended exposure.

• Electrostatic Sprayers & Foggers: After deep cleans, we apply hospital grade disinfectants using electrostatic sprayers to coat surfaces evenly. Fogging units emit a fine mist that penetrates fabrics and hard to reach areas.

• UV-C Light Sanitization: Portable ultraviolet C devices are used to sanitize interior surfaces. UV-C light destroys the DNA or RNA of pathogens and is routinely used in medical facilities. We employ UV-C only when vehicles are unoccupied and ensure that no passengers or chauffeurs are exposed.

• HEPA Grade Filtration: Select vehicles are equipped with HEPA or HEPA equivalent filters that capture fine particles and viruses. Filters are replaced frequently to maintain efficiency.

• Antimicrobial Coatings: We work with suppliers to apply long lasting antimicrobial coatings on frequently touched surfaces. These coatings inhibit microbial growth between cleanings.

Interior Detailing: Protecting Surfaces & Electronics

Cleaning soft materials requires special care. Leather seats are wiped with a microfiber cloth soaked in a mild detergent solution and then treated with a disinfectant; cloth upholstery is steam cleaned to remove dirt and pathogens. Steering wheels, shifter knobs, grab handles and door panels are wiped with quaternary ammonium. Touch screens are cleaned with at least 70% isopropyl alcohol sprayed onto a lint free cloth — never directly onto. We avoid bleach, hydrogen peroxide and ammonia based cleaners because they can damage interior.

Air Quality & HVAC Maintenance

We schedule more frequent replacement of cabin air and pollen filters — often weekly during high traffic periods and at least monthly otherwise. Air ducts and vents are disinfected using specialized sprays. HVAC systems undergo professional sanitization that removes biofilm and mold. Ozone and ionization treatments are performed after service intervals to neutralize odors and bacteria.

Driver Health & Personal Hygiene

Our chauffeurs undergo rigorous training on infectious disease prevention, personal hygiene and customer interaction. Key practices include:

  • Health Screening: Drivers self monitor symptoms, undergo daily temperature checks and stay home.
  • Hand Hygiene: Frequent hand washing or use of hand sanitizer between trips.
  • Physical Distancing: Whenever possible, chauffeurs maintain distance from passengers. Our booking system anonymizes order information to protect personal.
  • Professional Appearance: Drivers wear freshly laundered uniforms and maintain personal grooming standards. They do not wear heavy cologne or fragrances that could trigger sensitivities.

The Science of Clean: Global Best Practices

Our protocols draw from guidelines issued by health authorities, vehicle manufacturers and leading chauffeur services worldwide. Industry leaders recommend thoroughly cleaning vehicles before and after every job, disinfecting high touch points with EPA approved products, and offering hand sanitizer. Cleaning guidelines from vehicle manufacturers instruct us to open doors and windows for ventilation, wear disposable gloves when cleaning and discard gloves after. Interior surfaces should be cleaned with detergent before applying disinfectant, and quaternary ammonium cleaners are preferred for upholstered seats and plastics. High touch surfaces such as grab handles, seat belts, steering wheel, gear shift and air controls must be cleaned between trips. We also monitor emerging technologies like antimicrobial polymers, electrostatic coatings and aerosolized hydrogen peroxide systems. While some innovations (e.g., ozone generators) require caution due to potential respiratory irritation, they can be used safely when vehicles are unoccupied and properly ventilated.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Do I have to wear a mask during the ride?

We follow local regulations. At present, masks are optional, but we strongly recommend wearing them. Drivers wear masks when requested, and we provide masks, gloves and sanitizer on request.

How often are vehicles cleaned?

Vehicles are cleaned and disinfected before and after every ride. High touch surfaces are wiped down with quaternary ammonium disinfectant, and deep cleaning with SAO and ozone occurs regularly.

What cleaning products do you use?

We use EPA registered disinfectants approved for use against viruses and bacteria. Quaternary ammonium products are our primary choice. We avoid bleach or hydrogen peroxide because they can damage interiors.

What if I have allergies or chemical sensitivities?

Please notify us when booking. We can assign vehicles cleaned exclusively with fragrance free, hypoallergenic products and avoid ozone treatments. Our chauffeurs use non scented hand sanitizer and avoid colognes.

Can I book a bilingual or multilingual chauffeur?

Yes. Our team includes English, Hungarian, German, Spanish and Chinese speaking drivers. For other languages, please contact us in advance.
